Lamplight

Hand shadows that come alive on your wall

Sit facing a wall · put the controllers down · sound on

Lamplight is mixed reality, played with your bare hands on a Quest 3 or 3S. To play, open this page in the headset’s browser:

madhavvan.github.io/lamplight

Warming the lamp…

  1. A lantern arrivesIt settles over your shoulder and lights the wall in front of you.
  2. Raise your handsTheir shadows fall on your real wall, soft-edged like candlelight.
  3. Match the outlineMake the shape it asks for, and your shadow comes alive.
